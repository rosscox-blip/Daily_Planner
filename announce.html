Your Personal Job Portal
We've built a personal dashboard for each of you — a page you can access from anywhere (phone, home, wherever) that shows your active jobs, what's urgent, and key dates. All data comes directly from the team Google Sheet and refreshes automatically every hour. No more needing to ask Ross what's on your plate!
